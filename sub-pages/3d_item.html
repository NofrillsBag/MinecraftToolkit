Block Bench:


This is actually REALLY easy. After you install

Blockbench

Click on the ⋮ on the top. Search "Add Cube" in the

middle and click it

.

Add the cube

, with the new button. Look to the right on size, make it all 8.


Now go to paint, and click

Create Texture

, name it miner_talisman as well. Now, we just

texture this

; so I'm just going to scribble on it.

Then, go to DISPLAY, and we'll go through the options on the

left and tweak sizes and rotation

.
