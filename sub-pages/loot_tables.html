Random Loot

Chests:

Back on Misode, I went to LOOT TABLE generator.

So I am running through the

usual modifications

you'd want in a loot table;

amount of drops, random durability, random enchantments, name, and lore; and combining them.



For note, in this video. I edited COMPONENTS,

Components

are the heart of custom items in general, this lets you make custom items with effects & custom textures.

After creating the folder structure, and putting in the code. I gave myself chest;


/give @s chest[block_entity_data={id:"minecraft:chest", LootTable:"chocolate:chests/custom_chest_loot"}]





Entity Loot:

In vanilla, you can only OVERRIDE

existing loot tables

for entities.

Went back to the LOOT TABLE generator. And clicked entity.

I added leather, and zombie flesh, and in the folder I added

minecraft/loot_table/entities

. Not chocolate, since we are overridng MINECRAFT.

Then I just summoned the zombie, and boom there is our loot.
