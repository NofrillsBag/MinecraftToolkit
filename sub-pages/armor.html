Creating A Blank Datapack


This module is really simple, go into a world and type what is below in chat: 
/datapack create name "description"
(name must be lowercase, spaces are replaced with _)

To find it on your computer (So we can add stuff to our datapack), exit the world. Select your world in minecraft, click Edit at the bottom. Open World Folder. Then click datapacks; open in say VSCode.
