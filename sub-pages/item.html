Right Click Teleport:

Now this part is a lot, 7 new

"Minecraft Code"

files.

What we are trying to do, is

make the sword right click to teleport you forward 8 blocks, with a cooldown

.




The idea is:

We make an

achievement

that triggers when you

start eating the item

(right click), that has a reward that teleports you. It also starts a scoreboard timer to tick up, which resets every something seconds.





Go to data/chocolate, make two folders;

advancement and function

.
Inside function, make another folder called

aotv

.


↪

function/load.mcfunction

, gets another function create a scoreboard for the cooldown, when you LOAD the game.

↪

function/scores.mcfunction

, then makes that scoreboard cooldown.

↪

advancement/aotv_used.json

, sees that someone tried to use (eat) the aotv item, and rewards them with a function that starts the cooldown timer.

↪

aotv/activate.json

, checks the cooldown timer, and then gives the player their result, revokes the advancement to let them right click again.

↪

aotv/aotv_result.json

, is the actual teleport, starts the cooldown again.

↪

function/tick.mcfunction

, makes sure the cooldown timer always runs every TICK.

↪

function/timers.mcfunction

, just ticks down the clock.



↪

Lastly, go to the datapack's "minecraft" folder

, make a folder called

tags/function

. Add tick.json and load.json, with the videos code; the game calls the mcfunctions every tick, and every reload.
