Villages


More Folders:


For multi piece structures, we need to organize our structures; that means more folders.

Me personally, I'm going to make a

village in the sky

. It has a town center island, bridges, and house islands.

In the video, you see me adding my structure name (

sky_village

), then inside that folder I added my "pieces" street, town_centers, houses (our structure saves go here).

Then, I added a new folder inside template_pool called

sky_village

too.



The Minecraft Side:

Here (World Download Avaliable), I have my town_centers (beacon Island), my street (bridges) and my houses (other islands). How we connect them, is by a

Jigsaw Block.



In the video, I will put

Jigsaw Blocks

on the end of my streets, so eventually they can all connect, but for that to happen;

each piece needs to know:

"What am I doing?"




Connecting street, and town center:



I have 4 street types, which is all you need for a nice looking village, now I have to add the

Jigsaw Blocks

to the ends.

↪

I put

chocolate:sky_village/street in the first box the folder with all of our streets. This means, this block will look to spawn more streets.

↪

Then,

I put both chocolate:street as the name, and target name. This just means, I want streets to connect and spawn next to things named "chocolate:street".


Then, let's get an island in. Our town center spawns in first, so we just want it to spawn in and connect streets, we can copy the street

Jigsaw Block

and place it on all sides.


Already half way done.




Connecting bridges to other islands:



Let's go back to our streets; currently streets only spawn in and connect to other streets.

We want them to also spawn in and connect to houses.

↪

On a new side

put in the first box chocolate:sky_village/houses the folder we will store our houses in.

↪

On the 2nd and 3rd box,

put chocolate:housing_entrance, meaning we want it to spawn and connect to pieces named chocolate:housing_entrance.




The other islands:



Lastly, go to our other islands, the final piece.

↪

Place a jigsaw block on the entrance,

in the first box, do minecraft:empty, we don't want to spawn anything else from this island, it's ends here.

↪

On the 2nd and 3rd box,

copy & paste chocolate:housing_entrance, this means those street sides will connect to these islands.




LASTLY! We have to save all of our structures, with

structure blocks

. Use the naming scheme chocolate:name, chocolate:town_centers is my town center island for example.



Code:

These files and our code

don't change much

, from lone structures.

> You add a biome json for the biomes named sky_village.

> You add a structure set json for the math of the spawning

> You add a structure json for specific spawn settings, I have

two specific settings

for this.


↪

Remove

"project_start_to_heightmap" : "WORLD_SURFACE_WG" because it's in the sky, not ground.

↪

The line,

"start height, above bottom", spawns 214 blocks above -64 (bedrock) so Y 150.





LASTLY,

template_pool

, town_centers, houses, street are in seperate files.

It looks the same as the grave, template pool, the difference is it's now a list, and we have it in seperate files.

Done, go into a new world, place this new datapack in there, and spawn it with

/place structure chocolate:sky_vilage

.


Tweaks:



↪

Save Entities

to have villagers.

↪

The village is a little bit small

, ideally you want more islands and streets, you can do that by putting more street jigsaw blocks on the house islands (Yellow blocks on the world) then re-saving them into the pack.

↪

The island randomness

looks pretty static. Go back to template_pool and add some randomness to the "weight", and add an empty street, and if you want add the other house islands.

↪

Make sure

all of your house structure blocks have the structure blocks at the SAME Y level.
